2

Record the account-deletion screen recording

Apple's 5.1.1(v) explicitly requires a screen recording showing the full deletion flow. From their reviewer message:

"Reply to this message with a screen recording captured on a physical device that demonstrates:
- Creating a new account or signing in with the demo account
- Navigating to the account deletion option
- The complete account deletion flow from initiation to confirmation"
⚠ Important: Use a THROWAWAY account for the recording — NOT the reviewer demo creds (demo@bookatreatment.ai), because Apple's reviewer needs those creds to STILL WORK after you record. Suggestion: create test.delete@bookatreatment.ai via public signup, then record deleting it.
  1. Sign out if signed in
  2. Sign in with the throwaway account
  3. iOS Control Center → start screen recording
  4. Navigate to Settings → "Delete my account"
  5. Type DELETE in the modal
  6. Tap red "Delete account" button
  7. Wait for sign-out + redirect to login
  8. Stop recording
  9. Trim if needed in Photos → save .mov
  10. Upload to your preferred location (iCloud Drive, Google Drive, etc.) — you'll paste the link in step 5

4

Audit metadata on all 3 BAT app listings

Apple's 3.1.3(c) flag was partly about enterprise services being sold to consumers without IAP. Apple's reviewer probably noticed your consumer-facing BookATreatment app listing while reviewing the Admin app. Check all three:

  1. BookATreatment Admin — App Information · description should read "for clinic owners and staff," not consumer-targeted
  2. BookATreatment Staff (Apps list → click Staff) · same B2B language test
  3. BookATreatment (no suffix — consumer-facing, Apps list → click it) · if this is currently positioned for consumers, that's the surface Apple is flagging. Either:
    • Strip consumer language and re-position as B2B too, OR
    • Decide whether this app needs IAP (StoreKit) — but Option α (B2B-only) was the locked choice

Look for: "book your treatment," "find a clinic near you," "compare prices," "consumer reviews," "personal use," etc.
