1️⃣ Priority 1: Complete the Google Business Profile

This is the single biggest lever. A complete, detailed profile is what makes Google show the business to people searching nearby.

1Add real photos Luci

Photos of actual completed jobs, machinery on site, and the team at work. No stock photos, no AI-generated images. Even phone photos from recent jobs are exactly what's needed.

2Add each service with a description Luci

Add these as separate services in the profile, each with a short description:

  • Excavation, bulk and detailed excavation for residential and commercial sites
  • Pool Excavation
  • Site Cuts & Pad Preparation, preparing sites for new builds
  • Retaining Walls, engineered retaining walls
3Update the business description Luci

Paste this into the profile's description field:

First Light Civil is a licensed civil construction and excavation business serving Brisbane and the Moreton Bay Region. We handle bulk and detailed excavation, pool excavation, site cuts and pad preparation, and engineered retaining walls for residential and commercial projects. QBCC Licence No. 76041. With 12+ years of hands-on experience, our approach is simple: show up, work hard, and leave every site better than we found it.
4Confirm the category and details Luci

Check the category is the closest match to "Excavating Contractor" or "Earthmoving Contractor" (it may currently say "Earth works company", that's fine if it's the closest option offered). Confirm service area covers Brisbane and Moreton Bay, and hours are set (Mon to Sun, 6am to 6pm).

2️⃣ Priority 2: Get the First 5 to 10 Google Reviews

Zero reviews is the biggest gap versus every competitor. A handful of genuine reviews will do more for ranking than almost anything else on this list.

1Ask every recent and current client directly Luci

Text or email clients shortly after a job finishes, while it's fresh. A simple, low-pressure message works best:

Hi [Name], thanks again for choosing First Light Civil for your [excavation/site cut/retaining wall] job. If you have a couple of minutes, a quick Google review would really help us out: [Google review link]. Thanks so much!
2Get the review link Luci

In the Google Business Profile app or business.google.com, look for "Get more reviews" or "Ask for reviews" and copy the link it gives you, that's the one to send.

Keep it simple: one genuine ask per client, no incentives or discounts offered in exchange for a review (that breaches Google's rules). Just ask honestly and let the real experience speak.

3️⃣ Priority 3: Citations (list the business in the right places)

The same business details listed consistently across other sites builds trust with Google. Keep the name, phone, and details identical everywhere.

Business details to use everywhere
First Light Civil Phone: 0420 683 648 Email: firstlightcivil@gmail.com Website: https://www.firstlightcivil.com.au Service area: Brisbane & Moreton Bay Region Category: Excavating Contractor / Earthmoving Contractor QBCC Licence No. 76041
1List on these directories Luci
  • True Local
  • Yellow Pages
  • Hotfrog
  • Localsearch
  • hipages and Oneflare, these are trade lead sites specifically worth a listing for this kind of business

Use the exact same business details above on every listing.

2Bing Places Luci

Go to bingplaces.com, sign in (create a free Microsoft account if needed), and choose "Import from Google Business Profile", it pulls everything across automatically once the Google profile is complete.

3Apple Business Connect Luci

Go to businessconnect.apple.com, sign in with an Apple ID, and claim or add "First Light Civil". This gets the business into Apple Maps and Siri results too.

4️⃣ Next: A Real Project Gallery & Website Basics

1Collect project photos for the website Luci

Gather real before/after photos from recent jobs with a short note (suburb + what was done), e.g. "Site cut and pad prep, Redcliffe". These go on the website's gallery and can also be used as Google Business Profile photos.

2Add page titles, descriptions, and structured data Luci, in Lovable

Each website page needs a unique title and description mentioning "Brisbane" and "Moreton Bay" plus the specific service, and the site should have LocalBusiness/GeneralContractor schema with the QBCC licence number. In Lovable, this is a prompt to the site's chat asking it to add/update the page title, meta description, and schema for each page.

3A simple cost guide page Luci, in Lovable Check with Kim

People search "how much does excavation cost in Brisbane" a lot. A simple, honest page explaining what affects the price (soil type, access, size of job) without giving exact quotes captures that search traffic. Get Kim's sign-off on what to publish, then build it as a new page in Lovable.
