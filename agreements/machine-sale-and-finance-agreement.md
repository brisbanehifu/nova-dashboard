## 1. Definitions

**Financed Amount** — Total Price (ex GST) less the Deposit and less any GST paid separately.
**Delivery Date** — the date the Equipment is delivered or made available to the Buyer.
**PPSA** — *Personal Property Securities Act 2009* (Cth). **GST** — as defined in the *GST Act 1999* (Cth).

## 3. Option 1 — Outright Purchase
3.1 The Buyer pays the Total Price plus GST: a [50%] deposit on signing and the balance in cleared funds before the Delivery Date.
3.2 Title and risk pass to the Buyer on delivery, once the Total Price (incl. GST) is paid in full.

## 4. Option 2 — Vendor Finance (Profit-Share Repayment)
4.1 **Deposit.** The Buyer pays the Deposit plus the full GST on the Total Price on signing. (GST is payable up-front and is not financed.)
4.2 **Finance.** The Seller provides vendor finance for the Financed Amount on the terms in this clause.
4.3 **Interest.** Interest accrues on the outstanding Financed Amount at **25% per annum**, calculated monthly on the reducing balance from the Delivery Date.
4.4 **Profit-share repayment.** On or before the 14th of each month, the Buyer pays the Seller an amount equal to **25% of the Buyer's Net Clinic Profit** for the preceding month (the *Monthly Repayment*). Each Monthly Repayment is applied first to accrued interest, then to the Financed Amount.
4.5 **Minimum monthly repayment.** Each Monthly Repayment must be no less than $[MINIMUM] per month, regardless of profit, to ensure steady reduction of the balance. *(Set a floor, or delete if pure profit-share is intended.)*
4.6 **Target Term & Longstop.** The parties intend the balance to be repaid within approximately twelve (12) months. In any event, the entire outstanding balance (Financed Amount plus accrued interest) is **due and payable in full on the Longstop Date** ([18] months after the Delivery Date).
4.7 **Reporting.** So the Monthly Repayment can be verified, the Buyer must by the 7th of each month give the Seller (a) the prior month's profit-and-loss / management accounts, and (b) the prior month's business bank statements. The Seller may, on reasonable notice, audit the Buyer's records; if an audit reveals an underpayment over 5%, the Buyer bears the reasonable audit cost.
4.8 **Early repayment.** The Buyer may repay the outstanding balance early at any time with no penalty.
4.9 **Retention of title.** Title in the Equipment **remains with the Seller** until the Financed Amount and all interest are paid in full. Risk passes to the Buyer on delivery, and the Buyer must keep the Equipment insured for its full value from delivery until title passes.
4.10 **Business-purpose finance.** This finance is provided wholly and predominantly for **business purposes**. The Buyer must sign the **Business Purpose Declaration (Schedule C)** before any credit is provided. The parties intend this to be exempt credit under the National Credit Code.

## 5. Security
5.1 **PPSR.** The Buyer grants the Seller a security interest in the Equipment to secure the financed obligations and consents to the Seller registering it on the PPSR. The Buyer will do all things reasonably needed to perfect that interest and waives its right to receive certain notices under the PPSA to the extent permitted.
5.2 **Guarantee.** If the Buyer is a company, each director gives the personal guarantee in **Schedule D**.

## 6. Default
6.1 The Buyer is in default if it (a) fails to pay any amount when due, (b) fails to provide the reporting in clause 4.7, (c) removes, sells, encumbers or fails to insure the Equipment, or (d) becomes insolvent.
6.2 On default, the Seller may (after [7] days' written notice to remedy, except insolvency): declare the whole balance immediately due; charge default interest at [2]% per month on overdue amounts; and/or retake possession of the Equipment under its retained title and PPSR security. Repossession does not extinguish the Buyer's liability for any shortfall (net of resale proceeds) plus costs.

## 7. Warranty, Support & Training

7.2 Training is delivered at the Seller's Brisbane clinic per the package in Schedule A.
7.3 Nothing in this Agreement excludes the non-excludable consumer guarantees under the *Australian Consumer Law*.

## 8. GST

## Schedule B — Net Clinic Profit (for the Profit-Share Repayment)
**Net Clinic Profit** for a month means the Buyer's gross treatment and product revenue **received** that month, less the following, to the extent properly attributable to the Buyer's clinic and evidenced in its accounts (kept on Xero or equivalent):
- cost of goods sold (cartridges, consumables, retail stock);
- rent, utilities and premises outgoings;
- staff/contractor treatment wages or fees;
- marketing and advertising;
- merchant fees, booking-software and EFTPOS costs;
- insurance attributable to the clinic.

*Excludes* owner's drawings, income tax, depreciation of the financed Equipment, and any non-clinic income/costs. **Worked example (illustrative only):** month revenue $18,000 − costs $9,000 = Net Clinic Profit $9,000 → Monthly Repayment = 25% × $9,000 = **$2,250** (applied to interest then principal).

### Important Note — Legal Review
This draft reflects Queensland and federal law (National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009 / National Credit Code, PPSA 2009, ACL, Electronic Transactions (Qld) Act 2001) as a starting point. Before use, have a Queensland commercial lawyer confirm: **(a)** the business-purpose exemption genuinely applies and the Schedule C declaration is correctly worded and signed *before* credit is advanced; **(b)** the 25% p.a. interest + profit-share return is not at risk of being read down as a penalty or unconscionable; **(c)** PPSR registration is completed promptly (ideally before delivery) to protect priority; and **(d)** the profit-share definition is tight enough to prevent under-reporting.
