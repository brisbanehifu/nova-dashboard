## 3. Facebook promotion content — ready for Zeely AI

### 3a. Zeely brief (paste this into Zeely as your campaign setup)
> **Product:** HIFU treatment machines for beauty/skin clinics — sold as complete packages (machine + training + marketing support).
> **Goal:** Lead generation — get clinic owners and beauty therapists to book a 15-minute demo.
> **Audience:** Australian beauty therapists, salon/clinic owners, and aspiring clinic owners, mostly women 25–55.
> **Offer:** Packages from $19,995 ex GST. Two ways to buy: pay outright, or finance and repay from clinic profit. 2-year warranty, Australian support, hands-on training.
> **Tone:** Premium, warm, trustworthy — not cheap or hypey.
> **CTA:** "Book a free 15-minute demo."
> **Brand colours:** deep navy + warm gold. **Link:** hifumachinesales.com/hifu-machines

**A — Opportunity angle**

You don't need $20k in the bank to start a HIFU clinic. Low deposit, then repay your machine from clinic profit over ~12 months. 2-year warranty, Australian support, loan machine if yours ever needs service. Book a free 15-minute demo to run your numbers. 👇

Thinking about HIFU for your clinic? Buy from an Australian team that actually supports you: 2-year warranty, onshore support Mon–Sat, a hot-swap loan machine so you never lose a booking, and real hands-on training. Packages from $19,995. Book a demo. 👇

### 3e. Audience targeting (Meta)
- **Location:** Australia — start with QLD (Gold Coast, Brisbane), then expand nationally as it works.
- **Age/gender:** 25–55, women-weighted (don't exclude men).
- **Detailed targeting (interests):** Beauty salons, Beauty therapy, Aesthetics, Skin care, Dermal therapy, Cosmetology, Day spa, Anti-aging, Ultherapy, Small business owners, Salon (industry).
- **Behaviours:** Small business owners; Facebook Page admins.
- **Lookalike:** Once you have ~100+ leads in `/admin/leads`, upload them and build a 1–3% Lookalike — this usually becomes your best audience.
- **Retargeting:** Anyone who visited `/hifu-machines` but didn't submit the form (needs Meta Pixel on the Lovable site).

### 3f. Creative direction (for Zeely / image + video)
1. **Hero glamour shot** of the machine on a clean clinic bench, navy/gold tones, overlay: "Own a HIFU clinic — from $19,995."
2. **Treatment in action** — practitioner treating a relaxed client, overlay: "Add HIFU to your clinic."
3. **Before/after** carousel (use compliant, consented images only) with caption "Results your clients will book again for."
4. **Talking-head/UGC video** — you (Kim) 15–20s: "Hi, I'm Kim — here's everything you get when you start a HIFU clinic with us." (Highest-converting format on Meta right now.)
5. **Offer card** — "2 ways to buy: pay outright or repay from profit," navy background, gold accents.

> ⚠️ **Compliance note:** Meta restricts "before/after" body imagery and health claims. Keep claims soft ("popular non-invasive treatment"), avoid promising specific results, and get written consent for any client photos. Worth a quick check before the before/after creative goes live.
