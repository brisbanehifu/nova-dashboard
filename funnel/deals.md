- **RRP guide:** Face **$450 / 30 min** · Body **$500 / 30 min**.
- **Yield per cartridge set:** Face = **$44,500** / 100 treatments @200 shots per face cartridge (7 cartridges). Body = **$30,000** / treatments @500 shots per body cartridge (3 cartridges: 6/9/13mm).
