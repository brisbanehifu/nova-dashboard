How the move works (the money-saving play)

  1. For each domain you're keeping: first move its DNS onto Cloudflare (free), then transfer the registration to Cloudflare. A transfer adds 1 year to the current expiry, so you never lose paid time, and you pay Cloudflare's at-cost price instead of Hostinger's marked-up one.
  2. Turn Hostinger auto-renew OFF on domains as you line them up to move — so Hostinger never charges the dear renewal. ⚠️ Only do this once the move is planned, and never leave a keeper to lapse.
  3. Timing rules: can't transfer a domain within 60 days of registering/last transfer, and don't start a transfer in the final ~2 weeks before expiry (renew first if it's that close).
  4. The 5 .com.au domains can't go to Cloudflare — move those to a cheap Australian registrar (needs your ABN) or keep at Hostinger. See the registrar guide below.
  5. Stage it by due date — work the table top-down (soonest expiry first). No need to do all 30 at once.
