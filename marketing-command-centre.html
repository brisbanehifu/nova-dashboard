🎨 Canva Template Export Guide

For BookATreatment Marketing Hub — how to export so users can swap photos, edit text, and add their own logo.

⚠️ Why this matters: Single-layer PNGs only support text + logo + colour editing. To enable photo swap (the killer feature for clinics), each template needs 2 PNG files — a background + a transparent overlay.

📋 The 2-file export method

Step 1 — Export the full design (_bg.png)

  1. Open the Canva design
  2. Share → Download
  3. File type: PNG
  4. Size: ×1 (original)
  5. Transparent background: OFF
  6. Compress file: OFF
  7. Click Download
  8. Rename: templatename_bg.png

Step 2 — Hide the photo, export the overlay (_overlay.png)

  1. Open the Layers panel (Position → Layers, or Alt+Shift+L)
  2. Find the photo in the layer list → click the 👁 eye icon to hide it
    Alternative: click the photo and press Delete (undo afterwards to restore)
  3. Share → Download
  4. File type: PNG
  5. Size: ×1
  6. Transparent background: ON ✅ ← KEY CHANGE
  7. Compress file: OFF
  8. Click Download
  9. Rename: templatename_overlay.png
  10. Press Ctrl+Z to bring the photo back so the design is preserved
