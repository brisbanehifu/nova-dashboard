★Plain-English summary 3 min read

The plan: Brisbane HIFU's site has been rebuilt on Lovable, tracking is wired, foundational schema is live, and the 28 verbatim reviews + 12 blog posts have migrated cleanly. That's the floor — not the ceiling. To beat the corporate chains in Brisbane SERPs we now stack four levers in deliberate sequence: (1) harden the technical foundation, (2) dominate the local pack, (3) publish 40+ pieces of authoritative pillar/cluster content, (4) roll out a 20-suburb × 8-treatment programmatic matrix, (5) earn authority backlinks via digital PR, (6) convert the new traffic at higher rates with CRO, and (7) operationalise weekly/monthly maintenance.

1.3 Internal linking architecture — hub-and-spoke (3 hr)

Current Lovable nav is shallow. To pass PageRank deliberately we need a defined hub-and-spoke architecture:

Hub levelPagesInternal-link role
L0 — Root hubbrisbanehifu.com (home)Links out to all 8 treatment pages + top-5 suburb pages + reviews + about. Receives links from every other page (footer + sitewide breadcrumb root).
L1 — Treatment hubs (×8)/hifu-face-lifting, /hifu-neck-jawline, /hifu-body-contouring, /hifu-eye-brow, /hifu-mens, /hifu-post-pregnancy, /hifu-hand-decolletage, /hifu-dealsEach links to relevant suburb pages (e.g. /hifu-face-lifting links to /hifu-face-lifting/new-farm) + relevant pillars + clusters. Receives links from home + matching suburb pages + relevant blog clusters.
L2 — Suburb hubs (×20)/brisbane-hifu/new-farm, /brisbane-hifu/west-end, etc.Each links to all treatment pages offered in that suburb + the next-nearest 3 suburb pages (sibling linking). Receives links from home (top-5 only) + treatment pages + relevant clusters.
L1 — Pillar hubs (×10)10 long-form definitive guides (see Phase 3)Each pillar links to its 3–5 cluster posts + relevant treatment pages. Receives links from cluster posts (upward) + home (top-3 only) + footer's "Learn" menu.
L2 — Cluster posts (30+)30 blog posts (existing 12 + 18 new)Each links upward to its parent pillar + 1–2 sibling clusters + 1–2 treatment pages where contextually relevant.
L0 — Conversion pages/contact, /book, /reviews, /faqReceive links from every L1 + L2 page via sticky CTA. Pass minimal PageRank back (nofollow exit if needed).
Anchor text discipline: exact-match anchors are spam signals. Use descriptive 4–7 word anchors that include the target keyword and intent context. Example: ❌ "HIFU New Farm" → ✅ "non-surgical HIFU face lifting in New Farm".

1.4 Sitemap segmentation (1 hr)

Lovable generates a single sitemap.xml by default. For a site this size we want a sitemap index pointing at four child sitemaps so Google indexes them in priority order:

<!-- /sitemap.xml — index -->
<sitemapindex xmlns="http://www.sitemaps.org/schemas/sitemap/0.9">
  <sitemap><loc>https://brisbanehifu.com/sitemap-core.xml</loc></sitemap>
  <sitemap><loc>https://brisbanehifu.com/sitemap-treatments.xml</loc></sitemap>
  <sitemap><loc>https://brisbanehifu.com/sitemap-suburbs.xml</loc></sitemap>
  <sitemap><loc>https://brisbanehifu.com/sitemap-blog.xml</loc></sitemap>
</sitemapindex>

Child sitemaps contain: core (home, about, contact, deals, reviews, faq), treatments (8 treatment hubs), suburbs (20 suburb pages), blog (42 posts after Phase 3). Each entry includes <lastmod>, <changefreq>, and <priority> (1.0 for home, 0.9 for treatments, 0.8 for suburbs, 0.7 for blog).
