Step 0 — Fix the shop (do first, ~20 min in Shop Manager)

Check "Policy violations" FIRST. If there's an unresolved violation, Etsy hides your shop in search — that alone explains 1 view/week. Clear anything there before doing listings.
  • Resolve any Policy violations (left nav)
  • Fix shop location → Australia (currently Florida, USA)
  • Finish Settings (blue dot) + Finances to-do (tax/payment)
  • Answer the 2 unread Messages (response rate affects ranking)
  • Add a banner + logo + About story (Georgie Pinn's real artist credibility — World Science Festival, ISEA, projection art) and shop policies (digital = no returns)
  • Turn on Etsy's free AI-art disclosure properly; don't put "AI" in titles

Description (first line is what shows in Google & search)
Open with the searchable promise + who it's for, then theme story, then what they get (file sizes/formats), then "personalise it" upsell + care/printing tips. Mention inclusivity explicitly — it's your edge.
Photos
10 images: the art + 4–5 in-room / framed mockups (buyers must picture it on their wall) + a "what you get" graphic + a size guide. This is non-negotiable for art.

Pricing (per listing)

Digital download — the volume driver

Instant delivery, no fulfilment, ~100% margin. Lead with these to get sales + reviews fast.
US$15 generic · US$29–39 personalised

Canvas (add later via Gelato/Printful Etsy app)

Etsy connects to a print-on-demand partner that auto-ships to the buyer — same as the main store.
US$69 / 119 / 179 (sm/med/lg)

What Etsy actually costs you

To LIST = almost nothing

  • 📋 US$0.20 per listing, lasts 4 months (or until it sells), then auto-renews 20c.
  • So listing the 12 starter = US$2.40; 50 listings = US$10; 100 = US$20. That's it to be live.

Fees only when you SELL (~17%)

  • Transaction 6.5% + payment ~4% + A$0.25 + currency convert ~2.5% (USD→AUD) + the 20c listing renew.
  • On a US$15 download → ~US$2.45 fees → you keep ~US$12.55.
  • Opt OUT of Offsite Ads (you can, under US$10k/yr) to avoid the 15% offsite fee. Onsite Etsy Ads are optional (you set the budget).
Bottom line: ~US$10 to list 50 pieces, then ~17% in fees per sale. A digital download is ~100% margin to begin with, so you still net ~83%. Cheapest possible way to test what sells globally.
