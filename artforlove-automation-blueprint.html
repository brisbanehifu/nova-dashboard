Art For Love — Fulfilment & Automation Blueprint

How orders turn into delivered art with (almost) no hands. The key thing: this is off-the-shelf integrations, not custom code — so it's configuration + automation wiring, not months of dev.

The headline: ~95% of this runs itself.

Digital downloads = 100% automatic, forever. Canvas = 100% automatic after a one-time setup (a print partner prints & ships for you). The only human step is Georgie actually creating a personalised commission — and even that has automated intake, proofing and delivery around it.
