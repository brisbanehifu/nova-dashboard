Ad Campaign Revenue Model (Zeely-Style) 💰

How It Works

BAT.ai acts as an ad management intermediary. Users create Meta (Facebook + Instagram) ad campaigns through our Marketing Hub. We charge the user the Meta ad spend + an 8% service fee. Meta Marketing API is free — our fee is pure margin minus Stripe processing.

Example: User sets $100 ad budget for 4 days → Meta ad spend $100 + BAT.ai service fee $8 = $108 charged to user. BAT.ai retains $8. Net margin after Stripe: ~$7.50 (94% margin on fee).

Commission Trap
