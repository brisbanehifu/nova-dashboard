Art For Love

Complete rebuild — from dormant brochure to a shop that actually sells

The artwork is exceptional (479 pieces by acclaimed digital artist Georgie Pinn). The site isn't the problem's the art — it's that the current site doesn't let people buy. This is the rethink: turn the gallery into a frictionless store selling instant digital downloads and made-to-order canvas prints.

As at 18 Jun 2026 · live-crawled artforlove.shop · supersedes the 4-Jun Reignite tracker (folded in below)
The pivot in one line:

Stop asking visitors to commission a bespoke portrait (slow, high-commitment, no prices shown). Start letting them buy the beautiful art that already exists — download it in seconds, or order it as a canvas — with personalised commissions kept as a premium upsell.

Why it isn't selling — diagnosis (from the live site)

🚫 No prices, no cart

The homepage shows zero prices and no buy / add-to-cart button. It reads as a brochure, not a shop. Shoppers who can't see a price or a way to buy simply leave.

🐢 High-friction model

The only path is a bespoke commission — "upload your photo → we transform it → proof → print → ship." That's a big, slow commitment with no instant gratification and no impulse buy.

🧱 Weak platform

Built on Zyro (Hostinger's basic builder) — limited commerce, checkout, SEO and speed. And the hosting dies 2 Jul 2026, forcing the move anyway.

Net: a stunning catalogue of 479 artworks is sitting behind a wall with no price tag and no checkout. The fix isn't more art or more traffic first — it's making what's already there instantly buyable.

Why this fixes conversion

Friction removed

  • Price visible on every artwork — no "request a quote" wall
  • Buy in seconds (download) — impulse-friendly, giftable
  • No upload/proof needed for ready-made art = instant checkout
  • Canvas = print-on-demand → no stock, ships worldwide

The hidden asset: 479 artworks = an SEO machine

  • Each artwork becomes its own indexable product page
  • 11 themes → collection pages (Tribal, Viking, Punk, Royale…)
  • Inclusivity built-in (LGBTQ+, culturally diverse couples) = underserved, high-intent search niches
  • Occasion landing pages (anniversary, wedding, Valentine's) → gift buyers

🌍 Going global — what changes (and why it's a win)

Why global fits this model perfectly

  • Digital downloads are already global — instant, no shipping, no customs. The world's art buyers (US/EU/UK) dwarf AU. This is your main global engine.
  • Gelato prints canvases LOCALLY in 30+ countries — a US order prints in the US, EU in the EU. Fast, cheap, customs-free shipping worldwide at no extra cost to you. (Your "go global" Q just confirmed Gelato over a single AU lab.)
  • Inclusivity niche (LGBTQ+, diverse couples) is globally underserved — bigger audience, same differentiator.

What you actually change

  • Price in USD, not AUD — US is the biggest art market & sustains higher prices (bands below).
  • Tax = the one real new thing. Selling digital goods into EU/UK/US has VAT/sales-tax rules. Cleanest fix: run digital downloads through a Merchant-of-Record (Lemon Squeezy or Payhip) — they collect & remit ALL global tax for you (~5% fee), zero paperwork. Canvas tax stays simple.
  • Multi-currency: Shopify Markets shows shoppers local prices automatically (built-in, ~no extra cost).
  • New channel: Etsy — a huge built-in global audience for digital art, handles tax; great for instant sales while your own SEO builds.
Global pricing (USD) & cost impact: Digital US$15 generic / US$39 personalised · Canvas US$69 / 119 / 179 (sm/med/lg) · Commission US$99–179+. Cost impact is minimal: Shopify ~A$45/mo and Gelato are unchanged (Gelato global = no added cost); the only new cost is ~5% on digital sales if you use a Merchant-of-Record for tax (worth it — removes compliance risk), plus slightly higher international card fees. Same build, same platform — just USD pricing + a tax-handling layer for digital + optional Etsy channel.

Recommended build & fulfilment stack

Site / store ✅Shopify Basic (~A$45/mo) — recommended for a non-technical owner: cart, checkout, tax, orders, digital-download app + POD app all built-in & supported, no code to maintain. (Lovable+Stripe = no monthly but you'd build/maintain cart, checkout, delivery, tax yourself — only wins on pure fixed cost.)
PaymentsShopify Payments = no extra transaction fee (just 1.75%+30c card). Add Afterpay/Zip for canvases.
Digital deliveryShopify's official Digital Downloads app (FREE) for instant delivery. (Or Payhip — free→2% — if you want built-in image-watermark + PDF-stamping + auto-VAT.) Upload a watermarked low-res preview; clean high-res delivered on purchase.
Canvas print-on-demand ✅Gelato (recommended) — genuine custom sizes + local AU printing + free Shopify app + open API; auto-prints & ships to the customer, no stock. Add a premium Prodigi giclée line for gallery-quality. Both free to join, no minimums.
❌ Not CanvaCanva can't fulfil store orders (Canva Print = your own one-offs only). Keep Canva for mockups + social/marketing content (and Bulk Create) — design tool, not the order engine.

💰 Costs & pricing — researched 18 Jun 2026 (AUD)

What it costs to run (Shopify path)

  • Shopify Basic ~A$45/mo (annual + GST) — the only fixed cost
  • Digital Downloads app: FREE · Gelato/Prodigi POD app: FREE (pay only print cost per order)
  • Shopify Payments 1.75% + 30c per sale (no extra gateway fee)
  • Canvas print cost (~A$25/45/70 sm/med/lg) comes out of each sale, not overhead
  • ≈ A$45/mo all-in at zero sales — pays for itself in ~1 canvas/mo

Lean alt: Payhip (free→2%) for digital-only with built-in watermarking, no monthly — but no canvas POD, so Shopify wins for the full model.
