G2 + Capterra Strategy

Written: 2026-04-21 · For: Kim Klein

Tied to: COMPETITOR_RED_TEAM.md (free-money play #4) + CUSTOMERS.md (reviewer pipeline)

Status: Playbook — execute Phase 1 as a browser task before Phase 1 Batch 5 code work.

Defensive move first, marketing move second. BAT has no G2/Capterra profile today — that's exploitable for under $100 by any competitor or third-party aggregator. Claim now, reviews later.

1. Why this exists

The competitor red-team surfaced an uncomfortable truth: BAT has no G2 or Capterra profile today, and that's exploitable for under $100. Any competitor (Fresha, Timely, Mangomint) or third-party aggregator can:

  1. Create a lookalike/parody listing you don't control
  2. Pay to ensure their listing appears above your eventual one
  3. Buy "compare" ads against your brand once you exist

Claiming the profile now — even pre-launch — takes away that attack vector and gives you narrative control on day one.

The framing This is a defensive move first, marketing move second. Do it for the control, let reviews come later.

3. Phase 1 — Claim (45 min, this week)

3.1 G2 — step-by-step

  1. Go to https://www.g2.com/products/new (or login/create account first at g2.com/login)
  2. Search "BookATreatment" — if a stub exists (unlikely), click Claim. If not, Create New Product.
  3. Email verification: G2 requires a company-domain email. Use hello@bookatreatment.ai — it's Titan-hosted and works. Do NOT use gm.teamneo@gmail.com (personal/agency domain).
  4. Fill the listing with the copy in §4 below
  5. Assets to upload (prep first):
    • Logo: square PNG transparent bg, 400×400 minimum (pull from Logos and Branding/)
    • Hero banner: 1440×360
    • 3–5 screenshots: booking page, admin dashboard, calendar view, Marketing Hub (if not rolled-back), mobile booking flow
    • Short demo video (optional — don't hold up the claim for this)
  6. Submit for review — G2's team manually approves new listings. Takes 1–5 business days.

3.2 Capterra — step-by-step

  1. Go to https://www.capterra.com/vendors/sign-up
  2. Same email verification (hello@bookatreatment.ai)
  3. Capterra is owned by Gartner Digital Markets — claiming there also lists on Software Advice and GetApp (same ecosystem). One claim, three distribution points.
  4. Same listing copy applies (§4). Capterra has a slightly different field set; they'll prompt for "Deployment: Cloud/SaaS", "Training: Live Online", "Support: Email/Phone" etc. — all truthful, straightforward.

3.3 Don't forget the long tail

After G2 + Capterra + Software Advice + GetApp, the next tier:

  • TrustRadius — enterprise-ish, lower priority for clinic-SMB audience
  • Product Hunt — launch day only, different playbook entirely (save for actual public launch)
  • App Store / Play Store — mobile, separate workflow

5. Category selection

G2 categories to claim (priority order)

  1. Appointment Scheduling Software — the obvious one, highest buyer intent
  2. Spa Management Software or Salon Software — pick whichever has more searches
  3. Medical Practice Management — more competitive but high-intent for medical aesthetic clinics
  4. Booking Software — broader catch-all

G2 allows up to 3 primary categories. Pick 1 + 2 + (3 or 4) depending on availability.

Capterra categories

  1. Appointment Scheduling Software
  2. Salon Software
  3. Medical Spa Software (if the category exists — Capterra has more specific verticals than G2)
Anti-goal Avoid broad "CRM" or "Business Management" — too competitive, too generic, wastes the listing's discoverability budget.

7. Review-request templates

Email template (30 days post-live)

Subject: A quick favour that takes 5 min — would mean a lot

Hi [First name],

It's been about a month since you went live on BookATreatment.AI, and I wanted to say thanks for being one of our first customers. Your trust at this stage matters more than anything else we could do for marketing.

Speaking of which — would you be willing to leave a short review on G2?

Direct link: [personalised G2 review URL]

It takes about 5 minutes. The questions are simple — what problem did BAT solve for you, what surprised you, what would you tell a friend considering it. G2 verifies reviewers via LinkedIn or work email, so your review carries real weight.

No pressure, genuinely. But if you've got 5 min and you're happy with how things are going, it would help us enormously at this stage.

Either way — thank you.

[Your name]
BookATreatment.AI

Why this works: no sugarcoating, clear ask, explains why it matters, lowers friction (direct link + time estimate), acknowledges their time is valuable.

Anti-pattern Do NOT send a form-letter "please review us 5 stars" — G2 strips reviews that look solicited for rating. Ask for the review, let them rate honestly.

SMS backup

[First name] — quick favour? Left a G2 review for BAT would help us massively at this stage. 5 min, [link]. No stress if not. — Kim

When NOT to ask

  • Within 30 days of launch (not enough real usage)
  • During a support issue (never ask people currently frustrated)
  • Right after a price change (optics)
  • If they've just churned or threatened to

8. Defensive monitoring (weekly)

Create a recurring 15-min weekly check:

  • Google search "BookATreatment.AI" site:g2.com → any listings NOT from you? Dispute.
  • Google search "BookATreatment.AI" review → third-party aggregators, competitor comparison pages, Reddit threads
  • G2 dashboard → any new reviews? Respond within 48 hours (neutral tone, thank them, address specific feedback publicly)
  • Capterra dashboard → same
  • Competitor G2 pages (Fresha, Mindbody, Vagaro, Timely, Mangomint) → look for reviews mentioning pain points BAT solves → marketing fuel

Nova can spawn a weekly agent once scheduled-tasks MCP is wired. Until then, Monday calendar.

9. Paid tier decision framework

G2 paid ≈ $500–$1,500/mo. Capterra paid ≈ pay-per-click ads from ~$2–10 per click. Don't pay for either until:

ThresholdThen consider
Fewer than 10 reviewsDon't pay. Free tier is enough. Paid features are wasted without review critical mass.
10+ reviews, ranked in G2 GridConsider G2 paid to unlock badge rights + analytics. Likely worth it.
Measurable inbound leads from G2Test Capterra paid — pay-per-click at $2–10/click against your $29–199/mo product is a risk-managed experiment.
Competitor buying "compare" ads on your pageG2 paid mandatory — locks them out or matches their spend.
The honest version Most SaaS companies pay for G2/Capterra because their competitors do, not because the ROI is unambiguous. If you go paid, set a 90-day measurable goal (N qualified leads, M booked demos) and kill it if unmet.
