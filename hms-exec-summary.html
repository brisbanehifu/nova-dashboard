📡 Market intel — the "AI SEO" tool you flagged

That orange "AI SEO" product (keyword×suburb page generation, auto-deploy to WordPress, rank/social/YouTube rank, "Built by Australians") is a rival / reference, not in your stack. It's a programmatic doorway-page generator — bulk near-duplicate pages are what Google's doorway-page policy penalises, so it's risky to buy.

The leverage: for HMS (national B2B), suburb pages matter less than buyer-intent depth — and you've already built the right thing: a buyer's guide, "is a HIFU clinic profitable", "HIFU vs injectables", spec breakdowns. The win isn't 100 thin pages; it's getting these indexed (sitemap + GSC, above) and feeding new buyer-intent topics from the monthly ATP loop. Replicate the value, skip the penalty.
